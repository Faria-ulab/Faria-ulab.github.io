The Dual Lives Of Bangladeshi Young Footballer

By Faria Akter (223012075)

Photo Credit – Nayem Hossain

The person whose face appears in the photo is currently known as “Apu Roy.” In the Pabna district, Apu Roy lives. He wasn’t always like that, however. Due to the lapse of time, the demands of modern society, or a lack of a proper analysis, he has become a “barber”.

But in 2018, Apu Roy played on the Bangabandhu Gold Cup Football champions. The honor was given to him by the prime minister, Sheikh Hasina.

When asked about his present situation, he stated that he had to give up his dream of playing football and join other occupations in order to be alive in society due to a lack of finances and opportunities.


Photo credit - Mahmud Al Faisal

Bappy, a football player who is 22 years old, is also a student at a university. He has played football for many years.

His first success in a college competition, helps to earn his college a trophy. He says that he is satisfied with the facilities he gets from football, and he believes that if someone came to Bangladesh to make money from football, he would have nothing to do in Bangladesh other than giving up football. He is pleased with the benefits and respect he earned by football in his academic life as well as on the playground.
