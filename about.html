The Journey

My Story & Ambition

I am a passionate communicator and researcher focused on the intersection of media, society, and storytelling.

Over the past four years in the Media Studies and Journalism program at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB), I have cultivated a deep understanding of digital publishing, documentary research, and feature writing. My approach is rooted in rigorous fact-checking, strategic event coordination, and a relentless curiosity to understand human behavior in the digital age.

Ambition & Commitment

My ambition is to shape narratives that drive meaningful conversations. Whether I am analyzing historical archives for a documentary or coordinating large-scale cultural events like the Trashion Show, I am deeply committed to authenticity and impact. I believe that in an era of rapid digital transformation, well-researched and empathetically told stories are more crucial than ever.

Looking forward, I aim to leverage my diverse skill set in journalism, research, and project management to contribute to innovative media campaigns and educational initiatives that resonate with global audiences.
