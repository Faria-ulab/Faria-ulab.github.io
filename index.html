About Me

I am a passionate communicator and researcher focused on the intersection of media, society, and storytelling.

Over the past four years in the Media Studies and Journalism program at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh, I have cultivated a deep understanding of digital publishing, documentary research, and feature writing. My approach is rooted in rigorous fact-checking, strategic event coordination, and a relentless curiosity to understand human behavior in the digital age.

Whether I'm analyzing historical archives or coordinating large-scale cultural events, my goal is always to deliver impactful, engaging narratives that resonate with diverse audiences.
